title: 'Datasource-Driven Variant Assignment'
description: 'Configure CRM-driven Experiment assignment and understand its statistical implications'
---

Datasource assignment evaluates CRM conditions to assign customers to Experiment Variants without using random assignment in the Experiment.

## Prerequisites

- A published CRM Datasource must contain the customer identifier used by the Workflow.
- For a causal reading, use a field whose values do not change during the Experiment.
- For a causal reading, the field your conditions use must contain values assigned at random (see [Causal validity](#causal-validity)).
- Datasource assignment is available for Global holdouts and Continuous A/B tests, with **Per customer** randomisation.

For UI instructions, see [Assign Variants from a Datasource](/product-guide/analytics/experiments/datasource-assignment).

## Evaluation

Conditions are evaluated in the order configured, independently of the Variant order. The first condition met determines the customer's Variant.

Each Variant requires a condition with at least one rule. Rules within a condition can use **and/or** logic, as in a Condition Node.

<Info>
  There is no catch-all condition for all other Datasource rows. Write a condition for every group you want to classify explicitly.
</Info>

Date and datetime fields do not offer date-range operators in the condition editor.

## Worked example

Suppose a CRM field named `holdout_group` contains `control` or `treatment`, assigned at random. The Experiment is a Global holdout with an expected Control allocation of 10%.

| Condition | Variant |
|-----------|---------|
| `holdout_group = control` | Control |
| `holdout_group = treatment` | Treatment |

<Info>
  A row with an empty `holdout_group` matches neither condition. The customer receives the Treatment fallback and is excluded from results.
</Info>

Customers with no CRM row, anonymous customers, or customers whose CRM row cannot be read receive the Treatment fallback and are excluded from results.

The expected Control allocation of 10% is an estimate, not an assignment rule. The CRM field determines which Variant each classified customer receives.

## Unclassified customers

Customers are unclassified when they meet no condition, have no CRM row, are anonymous, or their CRM row cannot be read. They receive the fallback and are not counted in the results.

Results describe classified customers only.

| Experiment type | Fallback Variant |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Global holdout | Treatment |
| Continuous A/B test | Reference |

## Causal validity

Random assignment in the Experiment is the only method that guarantees a causal reading. Datasource assignment supports causal measurement only when the condition values were themselves assigned at random.

If a field reflects an existing segment, selection bias means observed differences cannot be attributed to the Experiment's Variants. Examples include opt-outs, a hand-picked population, a behavioural cohort, or a region.

Results depend on the observed number of customers in each Variant, not on the expected allocation. When statistical significance is available, a small Control takes longer to reach it.

Whatever the number of Variants, every group must come from the same random draw, and any other rule must be identical in every condition and combined with **and**, for the results to be causal. For example, adding `country = FR` to only `holdout_group = control` breaks comparability.

## Editing a live Experiment

The Datasource, conditions, and their order stay editable until the Experiment is concluded. Changes reach new traffic within about a minute.

A customer's Variant changes when you edit the conditions or when their CRM values change. In [Data Exploration](/product-guide/analytics/data-exploration), a customer who moved appears under each Variant they received, which blurs the difference between Variants. Moves also bias the comparison when they depend on the Variant a customer received. Avoid moving classified customers after start.

The assignment method itself cannot be changed after start.
