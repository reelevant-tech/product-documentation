A service account (a dedicated account for an integration) lets an integration connect without using a person's email address or password.

## Create a service account

Use a service account when an integration needs its own access. A role and at least one Team control what it can use.

![Users table showing the Create service account button and a service account row](/images/account/users-table.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Users">
    Go to **Account > Users**, then select **Create service account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose account details">
    Enter a **Name**, choose a **Role**, and select at least one **Team**. Choose a role that grants only the access the integration needs.

    ![Create service account form with Name, Role, and Teams fields](/images/account/create-service-account-modal.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the account and save its credentials">
    Select **Create account**. Reelevant creates the account and its first secret automatically.

    ![One-time view showing the client ID, client secret, and token URL](/images/account/credentials-view.png)

    Copy the credentials before leaving this screen. The **Client secret** is shown only once; you cannot reveal it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage client secrets

Select **Manage client secrets** from the service account row's actions menu. The drawer shows each secret's label, status, creation date, and last use.

![Client secrets drawer showing an active secret and its revoke action](/images/account/client-secrets-drawer.png)

To add a secret, select **Generate secret**, enter an optional **Label**, and select **Generate secret** again. Copy and store the new credentials when they appear.

You can have up to two active secrets. Create a replacement before revoking the current secret.

<Warning>
  Revoking a client secret immediately stops access tokens issued with it. Update the integration to use another secret before revoking the current one.
</Warning>
