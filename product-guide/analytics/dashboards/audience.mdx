## Overview

The Audience & Interactions dashboard focuses on the people behind the numbers — how many unique users see and interact with your content, and how engaged they are.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Reached Individuals** | Number of unique individuals reached on any Channel. |
| **Total Impressions** | Times a message containing at least one Reelevant Content was seen. |
| **Impressions Per Reached Individual** | Average impressions for each reached individual. |
| **Unique Clicker Count** | Number of unique people that interacted with Reelevant contents. |
| **Average Click Per User** | Number of times the same person clicks, on average. |
| **Click Rate (Unique Individuals)** | Unique clickers divided by reached individuals. |

## How to Read This Dashboard

- **Reached Individuals** compared with **Total Impressions** shows how often your audience sees messages again.
- **Impressions Per Reached Individual** can help you identify repeated exposure or possible fatigue.
- **Click Rate (Unique Individuals)** measures the share of reached individuals who clicked.
