## Overview

The Individualization dashboard measures how much of your content delivery is personalized. It compares **individualized content** (delivered through personalized [workflow branches](/product-guide/workflows/branches)) against **default content** (non-personalized fallbacks).

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Individualized Impressions** | Impressions where at least one individualized Content was seen. |
| **Individualized Display Count** | Number of individualized content displays. A content is considered individualized if it results from browsing interactions. |
| **Individualized Display Rate** | Share of individualized contents among all content displays. |
| **Reached Individuals (Individualized)** | Reached individuals who saw at least one individualized Content. |
| **Reached Individual Rate (Individualized)** | Share of reached individuals who saw individualized Content. |

## Individualized Content vs Default

The dashboard includes a comparison table showing side-by-side metrics for:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Individualized** | Content delivered through personalized workflow branches. |
| **Default** | Content delivered through non-personalized fallback branches. |
| **Total** | Combined metrics across both types. |

<Info>
  To increase your individualization rate, add more personalized branches to your [workflows](/product-guide/workflows/overview) using [data nodes](/product-guide/workflows/data-nodes) and [conditions](/product-guide/workflows/logic-nodes/conditions).
</Info>
